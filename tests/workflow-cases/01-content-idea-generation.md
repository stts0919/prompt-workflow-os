# Workflow Case — Content Idea Generation

## Setup

- Mode: `quick`.
- Workflow: `content-idea-generation` (001).

## Input

- Topic: SaaS pricing
- Audience: early-stage B2B SaaS founders
- Goal: educational content that drives newsletter signups

## Expected output

A prioritized idea list of 15+ items with score, angle, and a hook for the top 5. Each idea has explicit audience fit, novelty, and effort scores.

## Verification

- The output is in English.
- 15+ ideas generated.
- Top-5 hooks are concrete (not generic).
- Assumptions about audience are labeled.
- One suggested next workflow (`article-outline`, 006).

## Failure conditions

- Fewer than 10 ideas.
- No angle differentiation.
- Suggested pricing advice or invented statistics.
- Skipping the suggested next step.
