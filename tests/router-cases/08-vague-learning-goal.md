# Router Case — Vague Learning Goal

## Setup

- Mode: `guide`.
- Language: English.

## Input

> I want to learn more about product management.

## Expected routing

`learning-path` (085). Confirm level, time budget, and goal.

## Expected questions

1. "What is your current level (novice, mid-level PM, senior PM, switching in)?"
2. "How much time per week can you commit, and what is your goal (interview prep, on-the-job upskilling, a credential)?"

## Failure conditions

- Recommending a book list without a structured path.
- Producing a curriculum without asking about level or time.
- Inventing a specific company context.
