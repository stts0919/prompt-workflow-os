# Term Glossary — Simplified Chinese (`zh-CN`, Mainland China)

> Source-of-truth file for the `zh-CN` term glossary. Lives under `shared/locales/zh-CN/` per the architecture in [`../README.md`](../README.md).

This glossary is context-sensitive guidance, not blind replacement. Use it to
pick the Mainland term when the user's audience is Mainland China. User
preference, brand names, industry vocabulary, and required regulated wording
always override defaults.

The glossary is organized in 12 categories:

1. [Everyday digital and UI terms](#1-everyday-digital-and-ui-terms)
2. [AI and technology](#2-ai-and-technology)
3. [Business and strategy](#3-business-and-strategy)
4. [Marketing and content](#4-marketing-and-content)
5. [Social media platforms and content formats](#5-social-media-platforms-and-content-formats)
6. [Product, design, and UX](#6-product-design-and-ux)
7. [Research and data](#7-research-and-data)
8. [Project management and operations](#8-project-management-and-operations)
9. [E-commerce and customer support](#9-e-commerce-and-customer-support)
10. [Education and learning](#10-education-and-learning)
11. [Finance and business metrics](#11-finance-and-business-metrics)
12. [Common conversational expressions](#12-common-conversational-expressions)

## How to use this glossary

1. Detect the audience. If the user said or implied Mainland China (or
   Mainland audience), use this glossary. Otherwise use `zh-TW`.
2. Detect the channel. Channel-specific terms (小红书、公众号等) are listed in
   [5-6 categories](#5-social-media-platforms-and-content-formats).
3. Detect the user-recorded terms. If the user already used a Mainland term
   in their request (e.g. 「我想用软件」), keep their term.
4. Apply the substitution only to unprotected prose (see writing rules D).

## Limits

- This glossary does **not** claim to translate names of foreign brands or
  products (Google, Apple, Microsoft, NVIDIA, etc. stay in English).
- This glossary does **not** translate workflow IDs or code identifiers
  (`027 translation-localization` stays as-is).
- This glossary is a starting point, not exhaustive. Industry-specific terms
  (medical, legal, finance) require domain expertise on top of this list.

## 5. Social media platforms and content formats

Platform names are usually kept verbatim (or in their official Chinese form)
because they are brand names — but voice and tone conventions differ.

| Mainland (zh-CN) | Common brand name | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 微博 | Weibo | 短文 ≤ 140 字；话题标签 `#xxx` |
| 微信公众号 | WeChat Official Account | 长文 1500–4000 字，订阅制 |
| 微信视频号 | WeChat Channels | 短视频 |
| 小红书 | Xiaohongshu / RED | 生活方式种草 |
| 抖音 | Douyin | 短视频 / 直播 |
| 今日头条 | Toutiao | 信息流 |
| 哔哩哔哩 | Bilibili | 中长视频 / 知识向 |

| 快手 | Kuaishou | 短视频 / 下沉市场 |

LinkedIn, YouTube) keep their English names. If a Mainland audience sees
references to them, leave the names in English; do not translate to 「脸书」
「照片墙」 etc. unless the user explicitly asks.

| 感谢 | 谢谢 / 谢了 | 感谢、谨致谢意 |

## How to extend

When you add a term:

1. Decide the Mainland default by consulting at least one mainland-published
   reference (新华字典 / 现代汉语词典 / 主流大陆媒体用法).
2. Add the Taiwan / HK form for reverse lookup, even if it is uncommon.
3. Keep brand names in their official form (English or official Chinese).
4. Update both `shared/locales/zh-CN/TERM_GLOSSARY.md` and
   `shared/ZH_CN_TERM_GLOSSARY.md` (they mirror each other).
