# Term Glossary — `en-US` (English — United States)

> Source-of-truth file for the `en-US` term glossary. Lives under
> `shared/locales/en-US/` per the architecture in
> [`../README.md`](../README.md).

Context-sensitive guidance, not blind replacement. Use to pick the US
form when the audience is American. User preference, brand names,
industry vocabulary always override defaults.

10 categories, ~50 entries. Smaller than `zh-TW` / `zh-CN` because the
glossary focuses on US / UK spelling flips and US-specific idioms;
general English vocabulary is not catalogued.

## How to use this glossary

1. Detect the audience. If the user named United States or used US
   conventions, use this glossary.
2. Apply the substitution only to unprotected prose (see writing rules D).

## Limits

- This glossary does **not** translate brand names.
- This glossary does **not** translate workflow IDs or code identifiers.
- This glossary is a starting point, not exhaustive.

## How to extend

When adding a term:

1. Verify the US form by consulting at least one US-published
   reference (AP Stylebook, US government style guides, US academic
   style guides).
2. Add the UK form for cross-reference.
3. Keep brand names in their official form.
4. Update both `shared/locales/en-US/TERM_GLOSSARY.md` and the
   canonical map.
